<objective>
Refactor the app layout shell to be mobile-responsive: hide the desktop sidebar at mobile breakpoints, add a Sheet drawer triggered by a hamburger icon, and extract shared nav content into a reusable component.

Purpose: The existing `w-56 flex-shrink-0` sidebar occupies 224px on a 375px viewport, leaving ~150px for content — making the entire app unusable on mobile. This plan fixes the root cause by making the sidebar hidden below `md` and providing a Sheet-based mobile navigation.

Output: `sheet.tsx` (shadcn component), `NavContent.tsx` (shared nav), `layout.tsx` (refactored shell)
</objective>
